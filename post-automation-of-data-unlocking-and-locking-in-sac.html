    Automation of Data Unlocking and Locking in SAC

    Data locking in SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) alongside planning activities is essential to ensure data integrity throughout the planning cycle.

    Planning processes typically operate under strict timelines, and if data is not locked promptly after deadlines, there is a risk of unauthorized changes to planned data.

    We implemented automated data locking to help businesses eliminate such risks while substantially improving governance, accuracy, and operational efficiency.

    The Traditional Manual Approach

    Data locking is performed manually after each planning activity. However, this step can occasionally be overlooked, potentially resulting in data inconsistencies, compliance issues, or significant business impact.

    The Automated Workflow Solution

    Solution administrators advocate automating data locking based on the sequence of planning activities. This ensures data is secured at the appropriate stages seamlessly without requiring manual intervention.
