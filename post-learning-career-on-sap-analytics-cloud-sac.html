    Learning & Career on SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)

    Refer to the below image posted in linkedin by a Subject Matter Expert on SAC

    The above image is setting clear directions on the learning and career approach on SAC. We are very glad to say Our Trainings and Programs are almost aligned to the modes mentioned above.

    • Beginner's training is to reach Self-Mode
    • Advance training is to reach the Project-Mode
    • Aim of Hands-on Program is to give a Project experience and make the individual reach the consulting-Mode.
    • Our Inter-Program is more for resources who are freshers in SAC but have completed both Basic and Advanced training and want to have more hands-on on project scenarios and get acquainted with different project requirements. In due course, interns will be upskilled enough to work on SAC Projects.
    • Our Consulting Hours Program is to help the resources in Project-mode and Consulting-mode to be able to meet the complex and challenging business requirements.
