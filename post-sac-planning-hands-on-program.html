    SAC Planning Hands-on Program

    Refer to reaching Consultant-mode level in SAC in our blog post Learning & Career On SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)

    Our Trainings & Programs are sequenced to reach to the respective levels.

    View Our Trainings and Programs

    Hands-on program will contribute a lot to reach the Consultant-mode level.

    Most of the learners have a hard time gaining real project experience until they get an opportunity in the project.

    To give a real project experience through Hands-on program, we provide an end-to-end planning project hands-on experience on a Generic planning solution and part of FP&A (Financial Planning and Analysis) covering all the planning features.

    AIM of Hands-ON Program:

    The aim of this program is to take you through SAC planning functionality and use this knowledge in meeting your project requirement.

    • Generic Planning Hands-on experience: Participants come from different backgrounds, so a generic planning scenario (that can be understood by every participant, not specific to an area like Finance, Sales, Supply, HR etc) would be ideal to have a Hands-on. This program will upskill on how to build a custom planning solution irrespective of functional domain.
    • Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A): It is big and vast, hence we provide hands-on experience on a part of its process which will give a realistic business planning Hands-on experience for resources having FP&A background.
